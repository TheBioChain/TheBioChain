# The BioChain

**Infrastructure for biological data integrity.**

The BioChain builds the provenance layer beneath biological data — making the chain of custody from sample to publication independently verifiable, without requiring trust in any single operator.

Based in Oxford and Ghent. Active across archaeogenomics, livestock health, and the open standards that connect them.

## Active projects

### [`adna-provenance`](https://github.com/thebiochain/adna-provenance)
Independent reconstruction of archaeogenomic chains of custody using open verification tooling. Built on W3C PROV-O, SHACL, and the Poseidon framework. Trial code: `BCL-RES-AncientDNA`.

### [`pigware`](https://github.com/thebiochain/pigware)
Provenance-anchored behavioural and environmental monitoring on commercial pig units. Farmer-led UK pilot, Innovate UK-funded. Trial code: `BCL-PIG-001`.

## How we work

The BioChain Research operates on three tenets, consistently applied:

- **Provenance, not truth.** We record who made each claim, when, and on what authority. We do not certify that the underlying science is correct.
- **Open by default.** Outputs are published through bioRxiv, Zenodo, ResearchGate, GitHub, and The BioChain's public research channels. Software and verification tooling are released openly where appropriate.
- **Honest about gaps.** Negative results, failed approaches, and unresolved gaps are part of the scientific record — not material to be hidden.
